HMU Privacy Policy

Effective: 14 October 2026

HMU (HitMeUp) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect in the HMU app, how we use it, when we share it, and the choices you have over it.

1. Information we collect

We may collect and process the following information when you create an account or use the HMU app:

  • Profile data such as name, phone number, email address, and delivery details.
  • Order data such as your order history, delivery addresses, preferred restaurants, and support requests.
  • Account, device, and notification data needed to secure your account and deliver service updates.
  • Payment-related details needed to complete transactions through supported payment providers.
  • App usage and diagnostic information, such as app version, device type, crash reports, and connectivity information, to maintain and improve service quality.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your HMU account.
  • Process and track orders and deliveries.
  • Send order-related updates, service notifications, and account security notices.
  • Improve safety, support, customer service, and app reliability.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and unlawful activity.

3. Sharing of information

We may share information with trusted service providers and business partners that help us operate HMU, including:

  • Authentication and data hosting providers such as Supabase.
  • Messaging and push notification providers such as Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Delivery and order fulfilment partners, where necessary to complete an order.
  • Payment providers and support vendors who process transactions or assist with customer care.

We do not sell personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data retention and deletion

We retain personal data only as long as needed to provide the HMU service, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can permanently delete your HMU account from within the app at any time. After successful deletion, HMU removes your account and most user data from its active app database. Some data may remain in backup copies or with third-party providers under their own retention policies and legal obligations.

See the Account Deletion page for more details.

5. Security

We use reasonable organisational, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and rights

You may update or correct parts of your account details within the app when available. You may also request account deletion, or ask us for additional information about the data we hold, by contacting HMU Technical Support using the contact details listed in the app or on the Account Deletion page.
