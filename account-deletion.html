Delete your HMU account

HMU (HitMeUp) users can permanently delete their account and associated data from within the app.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to your HMU account.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Choose Delete Account.
  4. Review the information shown and confirm Delete permanently.

Account deletion is permanent. You will be signed out when deletion succeeds. If you cannot access your account, contact HMU Technical Support using one of the phone numbers below and ask for help deleting your account. Do not share your password or verification codes.

Data deleted

When account deletion is completed, HMU deletes the following data associated with your account from its active app database:

  • Your HMU login account and account/profile details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Your saved delivery location and address details.
  • Your restaurant ratings.
  • Your order history and associated order details stored by HMU.
  • Your account's push-notification registration data.

Data that may be retained

HMU does not retain your account, profile, ratings, or order history in its active database after a successful deletion. M-Pesa and other payment providers may retain transaction records under their own legal, operational, and privacy policies. HMU does not control those providers' records or retention periods; contact the relevant provider for details. Copies in infrastructure backups, if present, may remain until those backups expire under the provider's backup schedule.
